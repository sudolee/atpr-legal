# Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: 2026-09-27*

This policy covers the ATPR app (All-Time Personal Record), published by an individual developer.

### 1. Current version
The current version runs on your device and does not send your training data to us.

- **No account.** You do not sign in. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifier.
- **No third-party tracking.** This version does not include advertising, analytics, or attribution SDKs.
- **Local storage.** Plans, workout logs (exercise, sets, reps, weight, and RPE), personal records, estimated one-rep max, body weight, and sex (used for the DOTS score), plus settings such as weight unit, language, theme, rest timer, and barbell plates, stay in a database on the device. On iPhone, the rest timer may appear in a Live Activity on that device.
- **Device backup.** If you turn on iCloud Backup, or you back this device up to a computer, Apple may include the app’s local data in that backup under your Apple ID. We cannot read those backups. Removing the app deletes the copy stored in the app. A backup made earlier can still hold a copy until you delete that backup.

### 2. Future updates
A later version may sync your training data across Apple devices you own, through your own iCloud account.

- That feature is not in the current version. This version does not upload workout logs, body weight, or personal records.
- If we add it, those records would be stored in your iCloud account and synced only among Apple devices signed in with that same account. We do not plan to receive a copy on a server we operate, and we cannot read data kept in your private iCloud storage.
- Before the feature is released, we will update this policy with what is synced, how sync follows your iCloud settings, and how to delete the synced copy.

### 3. Children
ATPR is a strength-training log for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 4. Changes
We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change describes a new way your training data leaves the device, we will update this policy before that version is released.
